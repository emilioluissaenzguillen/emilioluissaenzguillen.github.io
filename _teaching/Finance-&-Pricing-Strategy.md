This module is designed to help marketers strengthen their financial and pricing skills to enhance their strategic influence within organisations. It focuses on strategic pricing and understanding key financial concepts from a marketing perspective, enabling students to interpret financial data, make informed pricing decisions, and improve cross-functional communication.
